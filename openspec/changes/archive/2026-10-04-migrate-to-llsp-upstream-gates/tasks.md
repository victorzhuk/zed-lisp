# Tasks

The recorded gate and version baseline for the migration onto `llsp`. Nothing here changes the extension, patches `llsp`, or clears a gate by writing it down: each row either observes the behavior a gate names, reads it from a source at the pinned commit, or records a fact published with the pinned release.

Preconditions, restated because they bound every row below:

- The pinned release is **`v0.2.1` (`436bc84`, published 2026-09-30)`** and the installed binary. This pin is the initial measurement, not a perpetual constraint on the migration. The parent's `v0.2.0` measurement is preserved as history in the record and is not a state any row may inherit.
- This child's record, [`gates.md`](gates.md), is authoritative for **G1 and G3** only, and holds the attributed current measurement plus the immutable history. G2 is authoritative in `migrate-to-llsp-cutover/design.md` under Identifier-map evidence, G4 in `migrate-to-llsp-host-feasibility/feasibility.md` under Gate consequence, G5 in `migrate-to-llsp-acceptance/acceptance.md` under Real-server proof status. This child's table records measured baseline findings and links to those owners; it does not hold another child's mutable state, and no other child writes here.
- A later measurement appends a superseding attributed section naming its own release; it does not rewrite a historical fact.
- The language identifier is sent on `didOpen` and never on `initialize`.
- No task below writes code, a configuration value, a dependency or a capability.
